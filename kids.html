⚠️ Tricky numbers to watch for

  • +422 — No country uses this. It’s not real. Don’t answer!
  • +1 809 / 829 / 849, 876, 268, 473 — Looks like USA, but it’s far away in the Caribbean. Calling back can cost a lot.
  • +232, +234, +269 — Often used for the one-ring trick. Don’t call back!
  • Any surprise call from overseas — let it go to voicemail. A real person will leave a message.
